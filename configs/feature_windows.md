# Feature Windows

The default SPIRAL feature windows match the paper analysis.

| Feature key | Detector | Center | Feature window | Blue continuum | Red continuum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `pah33` | D4 | 3.29 micron | 3.18-3.40 micron | 2.95-3.16 micron | 3.60-3.82 micron |
| `bralpha` | D5 | 4.052 micron | 4.00-4.10 micron | 3.84-3.98 micron | 4.13-4.30 micron |

The measured excess is the mean feature-window surface brightness above the
linearly interpolated local continuum, multiplied by the feature-window width.
